Safety is good for humans, by definition. And yet wearing a bike helmet feels "not cool". 

**Question:** Why are we attracted to things that are bad for us?
**Incorrect Answer:** because humans suck!!!!! 

⭐️ The real answer: we are NOT attracted to that which is bad for us.

Danger itself is NOT what we are attracted to. The _ability to take care of yourself in dangerous situations_ is what we're attracted to. 

We know that danger exists outside of our safe bubble, and we are attracted to those who can face it & survive. Those are the people we trust to keep us safe. Because they've proven it. No amount of rationalizing can change this felt truth.
