The God Conjecture proposes a solution for "the problem of evil"[^1], despite it being an open problem forever.

The solution is:

- Understand first the nature of "good"
- Now imagine a 100% "pure good" force. Pure love. Radiating, making everything perfectly beautiful wherever it goes
- It branches out and spreads and fills reality
- Any where that it cannot survive, or where it will cause harm/disharmony etc, it does not take those paths 
- BUT IT WILL NOT KILL THAT WHICH DECIDES TO CONTINUE TO EXIST along those paths
	- At that branching point, you have a thing that is 99% good
