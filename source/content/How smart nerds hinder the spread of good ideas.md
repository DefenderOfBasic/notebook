A smart nerd discovers idea X, like say, "prediction markets"

Idea X is a _really_ good idea. It's really useful. But normal people hate the _aesthetic_ of the nerds. They don't want to be like them. They don't want more of them. So they reject the idea.

_"Ad hominim!!!"_ cries the nerd. But it does not matter. The idea is rejected. The normies are safe from the tyranny of the rats.

**Centuries later**, a normie discovers the same idea. They call it `r/agedlikemilk` and `r/agedlikewine`. It's the same thing, but it has a better aesthetic. It feels good. It spreads. 

"we got it first!!!" cry the nerds. But it does not matter. Each culture gets to write its own history. This is a feature, not a bug.
