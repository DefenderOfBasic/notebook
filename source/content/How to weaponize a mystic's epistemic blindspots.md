A "mystic" is someone who believes in "rigorous intuition". As in, _"I feel like theory A is true"_ is sufficient evidence to put resources into proving theory A. 

This is categorically different from a "rationalist" approach of feeling -> logical evidence -> persuasion. 

This creates an epistemic "hole" for mystics: you can just show up in their community and start saying "I feel"
